PeakCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

PeakCut is a tool that cuts long videos into short clips and posts them to your own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This page explains what it does with your information.

What we access

When you connect a social account, PeakCut asks the platform for permission to read your basic profile (your account name) and to publish videos on your behalf. We never see or store your password. You sign in on the platform's own page.

What we store

PeakCut stores the access token the platform gives us so it can post your clips. Tokens and videos are kept on the machine where PeakCut runs. We do not sell, share or send your data to anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we post

PeakCut only posts clips you have chosen to publish, to the accounts you have connected.

Removing access

You can disconnect an account inside PeakCut at any time, or revoke PeakCut's access from your YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account settings. Once access is revoked, PeakCut can no longer post to that account. Deleting PeakCut's local data removes the stored tokens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
